🔬 Methodology

Honest numbers, transparent math, cited sources.

⚡ Energy

E = Efixed + ein·Tin + eout·Tout

Each model’s three published benchmark points (short / medium / long, Jegham et al. 2025) are fitted to a per-token linear model at load time. The fit consistently shows output tokens dominate — generating text is sequential while reading your prompt is massively parallel. That’s why asking for shorter answers is the single most effective habit: halving the answer length roughly halves the energy.

💧 Water

W = (E / PUE)·WUEsite + E·WUEsource

On-site cooling water applies to IT energy; off-site water is consumed generating the electricity itself — and often exceeds the cooling share. We include both, unlike most provider disclosures.

🌍 Carbon

C = E · CIF · 1.15

Energy times the grid’s carbon intensity (your selected region, or the host’s grid), with a 15% embodied uplift amortizing chip manufacturing and data-center construction — the lifecycle boundary used by Google’s 2025 Gemini analysis and Mistral’s LCA.

🏷️ Eco-grades

EU-energy-label-style bands on per-query energy, anchored so a median 2025 assistant prompt (Google-reported 0.24 Wh, OpenAI 0.34 Wh) lands in A:

⚠️ Limitations

Per-query AI footprints carry real uncertainty: providers disclose medians not distributions, hardware and batching vary, and reasoning-mode token budgets are contested (estimates for one GPT-5 Thinking answer range from a few Wh to ~40 Wh). We show confidence ranges, widen them when extrapolating, and label every model’s data source. Treat the numbers as honest order-of-magnitude guidance, not invoices.
